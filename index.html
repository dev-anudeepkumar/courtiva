About Us

Courtiva is about the feeling when you are on the court, feeling like you are in the right place at the right time and you were meant to be here. It is about showing up for yourself. There is no other place you would rather be. It is about imbibing this spirit when you go to the court, on court and off court.

Courtiva is about that feeling of stepping onto the court and knowing there is nowhere else you'd rather be.

That's not just about tennis. It's about belonging, presence, and joy – and those are emotions that can carry Courtiva far beyond apparel.

More Than What You Wear.

Courtiva is more than a name. It's a feeling.

For me, the tennis court has always been more than a place to play. It's where I feel most like myself – where everyday noise fades, friendships grow, challenges become rewarding, and I leave feeling stronger, both physically and mentally.

That feeling inspired Courtiva. What began as the idea of being a diva on court evolved into something deeper: showing up as your best self – confident, graceful, comfortable, and completely present. As I played more, I realised that the experience didn't end with the final point. Hours in the sun, long rallies, tired muscles, and an active lifestyle made me look for something beyond performance – sportswear that also cared about comfort, recovery, elegance, and how a woman feels.

That became Courtiva. Every piece is thoughtfully designed to support women before, during, and after their game – combining performance with comfort and a sense of luxury. Because luxury isn't simply about what you wear. It's about how it makes you feel. Courtiva is for women who play because they love the game. Who value wellness as much as performance. Who find joy in movement, friendship, competition, laughter, and the moments in between. Move beautifully. Play confidently. Feel like yourself. That is the spirit of Courtiva.

– Kashmira

Founder, Courtiva
