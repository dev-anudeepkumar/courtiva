Founder Story

Some journeys begin with a single decision that quietly changes everything. Mine began after the birth of my second son, when I found myself carrying more weight than I ever had – and, rather than turning to a gym, I turned instead to a sport I had long admired from a distance but never dared to learn: tennis.

What started as a personal challenge soon became an all-consuming passion. I was rarely the best player on the court, but that was never the point – the game itself was enough. I played through Singapore's relentless heat, undeterred by concerns from my husband and mother about its effect on my skin. Day or night, I was there. Every other form of exercise fell away; tennis became the only one that mattered.

Yet with that devotion came a quieter truth. I would leave the court stiff, fatigued, drained by hours of play in punishing heat – and I began to notice how difficult it was to carry that exhaustion gracefully into the rest of my life.

Long before tennis, another world had shaped me: luxury fashion. Years spent within the industry had instilled in me a deep appreciation for design, craftsmanship, and the quiet power of things made with intention.

It was at the intersection of these two worlds that Courtiva began to take form. During the ESSEC Executive Master in Luxury Management and Design Innovation (EMiLUX), I spent a year studying how the world's most storied luxury houses use design and innovation to elevate the everyday. And as a woman playing tennis regularly, I saw with increasing clarity what was missing from my own sport: apparel that was as beautifully considered as it was genuinely functional – designed not only to look elegant, but to support comfort, wellness, and recovery.

That insight never left me. EMiLUX gave it shape, and the confidence to build something from it.

Courtiva is the result – luxury tenniswear crafted for the modern woman who moves with purpose, plays without compromise, and feels her best, on and off the court.

– Founder

Our Philosophy

Recovery is the New Luxury.

Courtiva was born from a simple realization.

As women, we don't play tennis simply to compete. We play to move, to reconnect with ourselves, to spend time with friends, to challenge our bodies, and to invest in our well-being.

Yet after hours on the court – especially in tropical climates – the effects often linger long after the final point. Fatigue. Muscle soreness. Heat exhaustion. Dehydrated skin. Stiffness. The body works hard, but most tennis apparel is designed to help us perform, not to help us recover.

At Courtiva, we believe that should change.

We are redefining luxury sportswear by designing every piece with one purpose in mind: to support how women feel during and after they play.

Because true luxury isn't measured by a logo. It's measured by comfort. It's measured by well-being. It's measured by how effortlessly you move – and how beautifully you recover.

Our Fabric Story

Thoughtfully Engineered for Recovery.

Every Courtiva garment begins with fabric. Not because it's the most visible part of the design – but because it's the part that stays with you long after the match is over.

Our collection is crafted using premium performance textiles selected for their exceptional softness, breathability, moisture management, and luxurious hand feel.

At the heart of our innovation is Far Infrared (FIR) technology. Mineral-infused yarns are woven directly into the fabric, allowing it to absorb your body's natural warmth and gently re-emit far infrared energy back towards the skin. Unlike topical treatments or coatings, the technology is embedded within the fibres themselves.

Research suggests that FIR textiles may help support healthy microcirculation, ease muscle tension, enhance thermal comfort, and contribute to post-activity recovery, making them an ideal choice for active lifestyles.

While Courtiva garments are not medical products, we believe thoughtful textile innovation can make a meaningful difference to how women experience movement, recovery, and everyday comfort.

Beyond Performance

The world doesn't need another performance brand. There are already countless brands designed to help athletes run faster, jump higher, or hit harder.

Courtiva was created for something different – for the woman who plays because tennis makes her feel strong. Because movement is her form of self-care. Because wellness isn't a trend – it's a way of living. Because she values craftsmanship as much as functionality.

She doesn't need to choose between elegance and innovation. She deserves both.

Designed for Life On and Off the Court

Every silhouette is intentionally created to move effortlessly between sport and lifestyle. Elegant enough for coffee after a match. Comfortable enough for long mornings at the club. Technical enough to perform on court. Luxurious enough to wear beyond it.

Because recovery doesn't begin after the game. It begins with what you choose to wear.

The Courtiva Promise

We believe the future of luxury sportswear isn't about pushing harder. It's about recovering smarter. It's about creating garments that care for the women who wear them.

It is where thoughtful design meets innovative textiles. Where performance meets well-being. Where luxury meets purpose.
