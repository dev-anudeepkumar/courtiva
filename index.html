Explore The Story

From Singapore Courts to ESSEC Paris

Some journeys begin with a single decision that quietly changes everything. Mine began after the birth of my second son, when I turned – rather than to a gym – to a sport I had long admired but never dared to learn: tennis.

What started as a personal challenge soon became an all-consuming passion. I played through Singapore's relentless heat, day or night. Yet with that devotion came a quieter truth: I would leave the court stiff and drained, and I began to notice how difficult it was to carry that exhaustion gracefully into the rest of my life.

Long before tennis, luxury fashion had shaped me – years in the industry instilled a deep appreciation for design, craftsmanship, and the quiet power of things made with intention. During the ESSEC Executive Master in Luxury Management and Design Innovation (EMiLUX), I spent a year studying how the world's most storied luxury houses elevate the everyday. As a woman playing tennis regularly, I saw what was missing from my own sport: apparel as beautifully considered as it was genuinely functional.

Courtiva is the result – luxury tenniswear crafted for the modern woman who moves with purpose, plays without compromise, and feels her best, on and off the court.
